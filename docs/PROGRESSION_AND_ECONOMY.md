# Progression and Economy

## Progression

Ezra develops through practical skills, relationships, route knowledge, evidence literacy, toolmaking, navigation, and leadership. Progression must not turn him into a superhero.

## Campaign resources

Food, medicine, tools, paper, ink, ammunition, money, transport condition, workshop capacity, refuge capacity, and evidence integrity.

## Economic representation

The cotton and slave system appears through land, debt, production, storage, transport, port commerce, insurance, legal claims, and labour coercion. These systems are represented critically, not as an opportunity for neutral player enrichment.

## Trade-offs

Protecting a refuge may consume medicine. Publishing evidence may increase pursuit. Sabotaging a wagon may reduce transport capacity. Saving a document may delay an evacuation. All gameplay-critical trade-offs are deterministic and testable.
