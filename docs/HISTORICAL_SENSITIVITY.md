# Historical Sensitivity

## Principles

Do not romanticize slavery. Show humanity, expertise, family bonds, culture, agency, resistance, and internal disagreement. Also show law, finance, property claims, land, labour, transport, policing, and violence.

## Representation safeguards

- Do not make Ezra a replacement for a historical freedom fighter.
- Do not make white allies the centre of liberation.
- Do not write enslaved characters as passive until Ezra arrives.
- Do not use historical figures as disposable cameos.
- Do not use slurs casually.
- Do not make suffering a collectible or spectacle.

## Consultation

Plan review with historians, Black history specialists, sensitivity readers, language consultants, and accessibility specialists.

## Accessibility

Provide controls and warnings for violence, family separation, pursuit dogs, captivity, racial violence, and slurs. Warnings must be specific and configurable.
