# Map and Regions

## Scale

Target playable area: approximately 45 square miles / 117 square kilometres across five connected regions. The map prioritizes meaningful travel, route discovery, environmental identity, and systemic density over geographic simulation.

## BC_REGION_SOUTHAMPTON_VA

Locations: Ashgrove Plantation, farms, estates, courthouse, patrol roads, creeks, forests. Focus: observation, sabotage, family separation, and escape preparation.

## BC_REGION_GREAT_DISMAL_SWAMP

Locations: maroon refuge communities, canoe routes, hidden camps, fishing and medicine sites. Focus: concealment, navigation, survival, and refuge management.

## BC_REGION_COASTAL_CAROLINA

Locations: river towns, farms, ferries, warehouses, border settlements, smuggling routes. Focus: escort, documents, river travel, and safe-house logistics.

## BC_REGION_NEW_ORLEANS_DELTA

Locations: port, commercial district, river traffic, warehouses, plantations, free communities, multilingual neighbourhoods. Focus: infiltration, commerce, evidence, and trader resolution.

## BC_REGION_NORTHERN_CORRIDOR

Locations: farms, workshops, safe houses, printers, political and newspaper contacts. Focus: diplomacy, intelligence, recruitment, and coordination.

## Transport

Horse, wagon, riverboat, canoe, and coastal vessel. Travel outcomes use date, route, weather state, transport state, alert state, and evidence profile as deterministic inputs.
