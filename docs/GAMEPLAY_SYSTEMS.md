# Gameplay Systems

## Reputation

Track separate reputation for enslaved communities, free Black communities, the escape network, merchants, plantation authorities, patrols, and northern political contacts.

## Pursuit

Bounty begins after the first escape. Alert spreads through witnesses, informants, patrols, newspapers, and evidence exposure. Pursuit uses deterministic inputs: date, region, alert state, route, weather state, and evidence profile.

## Documents

Work passes, river permits, merchant identities, abolitionist letters, forged plantation documents, and disguises have fixed validity, region, issuer, and inspection risk.

## Resources

Money, food, medicine, ammunition, tools, cloth, paper, ink, horse condition, boat condition, workshop state, safe-house capacity, and rescue transport capacity.

## Rescue ledger

Each rescued person has a stable identity, destination, food requirement, medical requirement, transport requirement, shelter requirement, protection requirement, and relationship state. People are not collectibles.

## Combat

Combat is dangerous, limited, and context-sensitive. Violence creates witnesses, injuries, bounty, political consequences, and resource costs. Ezra is not a superhuman gunfighter.

## Economy

The game represents plantation production, land, credit, storage, river transport, port export, insurance, debt, auctions, trafficking, newspapers, and political influence. The player cannot treat exploitation as a neutral profit strategy.
