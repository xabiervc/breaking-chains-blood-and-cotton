# Dialogue and Languages

## Voice and subtitles

Primary voice language: English. Recommended launch subtitles and interface languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Korean.

## Dialogue principles

Use understandable period-aware English without artificial archaism or caricatured accents. Give enslaved, free Black, white abolitionist, Indigenous, immigrant, and working-class characters independent goals and perspectives. Avoid casual use of slurs; where historically necessary, provide content warnings and narrative purpose.

## Narrative perspective

Do not make white allies the moral centre. Let communities disagree about secrecy, violence, evacuation, evidence, and survival. Dialogue must not imply that Ezra is the only person capable of resistance.

## Localization

Dialogue IDs, speaker IDs, context flags, content warnings, and historical classification must be stable. Translations must preserve agency, register, and ambiguity.
