# Historical Research

## Research policy

Every historical claim must have a source record with title, author or institution, publication date where available, URL or archive reference, accessed date, claim supported, confidence, and classification.

## Required research areas

- Southampton County and the 1831 rebellion aftermath.
- Virginia legislation and public debate in 1831–1832.
- Slave patrols and militia structures.
- Enslaved skilled labour and blacksmithing.
- Domestic slave trade routes from the Upper South to the Deep South.
- New Orleans port commerce and trafficking.
- Free Black communities and mutual aid.
- Escape networks and the limitations of the Underground Railroad label.
- Quaker, church, newspaper, and abolitionist activity.
- Period transport, tools, weapons, clothing, food, medicine, and language.

## Current historical boundary

The fictional Ashgrove network, Elias Vane, direct plot, named targets, and Ezra's campaign are fiction. They must not be cited as historical facts.
