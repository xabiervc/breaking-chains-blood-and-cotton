# Side Missions

Side missions expand the world without changing the fixed main ending. They must give communities goals beyond assisting Ezra.

## Categories

- Family separation and reunification.
- Medical care and supply gathering.
- Toolmaking and repair.
- River and wagon escort.
- Document recovery and verification.
- Community disputes.
- Safe-house relocation.
- Printing and testimony.
- Patrol evasion.
- Burial, mourning, and memory.

## Design rules

Side missions may alter resources, reputation, route safety, evidence quality, refuge capacity, and character relationships. They may not erase the national historical context or turn rescued people into collectibles.

Every side mission must state whether it is `FACT`, `RECONSTRUCTION`, `FICTION`, or `OPEN_QUESTION`.
