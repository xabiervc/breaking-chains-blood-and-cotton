# Activities and Free Roam

## Activities

- Blacksmithing, wagon repair, and tool maintenance.
- Fishing, hunting, farming support, and gathering.
- Canoe and river navigation.
- Route scouting and patrol observation.
- Document copying, reading, and verification.
- Community meetings and testimony collection.
- Printing, distribution, and rumour management.
- Medical preparation and refuge maintenance.

## Design principle

Activities show knowledge, labour, mutual dependence, and risk. They must not turn the plantation economy or human trafficking into a neutral optimization game.

## Determinism

Activity outcomes are determined by skill state, tools, resources, date, location, weather state, alert state, and documented prerequisites. Cosmetic ambience may vary within a seeded state.
