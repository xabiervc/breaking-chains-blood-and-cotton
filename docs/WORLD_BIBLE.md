# World Bible

## Setting

The game is set in the antebellum United States from 1830 to 1834. The opening takes place in fictional Ashgrove Plantation in Southampton County, Virginia, after the August 1831 Southampton uprising led by Nat Turner.

## World rules

Travel takes time and consumes resources. Weather affects tracking, visibility, river travel, and patrol behaviour. NPC schedules and market values are date-seeded and deterministic. Fast travel requires discovered routes and reliable contacts.

## Historical layers

- `FACT`: documented places, institutions, dates, and economic structures.
- `RECONSTRUCTION`: scholarly interpretation of practices where evidence is incomplete.
- `FICTION`: Ezra, Ashgrove, direct antagonists, the regional network, and plot events.
- `OPEN_QUESTION`: unresolved or contested details requiring research.

## Tone

The world is serious, human, and materially specific. It shows expertise, family bonds, humour, faith, work, fear, resistance, and disagreement alongside coercion and violence. It must not romanticize slavery or make suffering a collectible spectacle.

## Regional identity

Virginia brings surveillance and plantation discipline. The Great Dismal Swamp brings concealment, navigation, medicine, and refuge. Coastal Carolina and river routes bring logistics and smuggling. New Orleans brings port commerce and urban infiltration. The northern corridor brings printing, diplomacy, and supply coordination.
