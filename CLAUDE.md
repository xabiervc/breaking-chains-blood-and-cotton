# Repository Working Agreement

## Scope

This repository documents and prototypes Breaking Chains: Blood and Cotton.

## Non-negotiable canon

Preserve the setting, period, protagonist, four technical acts, five regions, 340-person regional liberation total, fictional Ashgrove Plantation, fictional direct plot, and regional ending.

## Historical handling

Label content as `FACT`, `RECONSTRUCTION`, `FICTION`, or `OPEN_QUESTION`. Source historical claims. Never invent quotations. Do not use historical figures as disposable cameos or replace them with Ezra.

## Representation

Give enslaved and free Black characters independent goals and agency. Do not make white allies the moral centre. Do not reduce rescued people to collectibles. Do not use slurs casually.

## Determinism

Main mission outcomes, named-character survival, dates, required routes, rescue identities, and final outcomes must be deterministic. Cosmetic ambience may vary only within a date-seeded state.

## Stable IDs

Use prefixes such as `BC_ACT`, `BC_MISSION`, `BC_CHAR`, `BC_FACTION`, `BC_REGION`, `BC_LOCATION`, `BC_EVIDENCE`, `BC_ROUTE`, and `BC_RESCUE`. IDs are immutable after publication.

## Documentation response format

When proposing repository work, report: files inspected, changes made, canon preserved, new IDs, deterministic rules, sources needed, tests added, unresolved questions, and next implementation phase.
